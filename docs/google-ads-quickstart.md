# Google Ads Quick-Start — "Built with AI Canada"

The **only** way to appear at the top of Google *today* for these searches is a paid
Search ad. Organic SEO (already deployed) is the free long game; this is the fast lane.
Once a campaign is approved (usually a few hours), your ad shows above the organic
results the moment someone searches your keywords.

**Setup time:** ~10–15 minutes at <https://ads.google.com>
**Send all ad traffic to:** `https://builtwithai.ca/ai-consulting-canada.html`

---

## 1. Campaign settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | **Search** (not "Performance Max" — you want control) |
| Goal | Leads / Website traffic |
| Networks | **Uncheck** "Search partners" and "Display network" (Google Search only) |
| Location | Canada (or just your province/city to start cheaper, e.g. Saskatchewan / Regina) |
| Language | English |
| Budget | Start at **$10–20 CAD/day**, adjust after a week |
| Bidding | "Maximize clicks" with a **max CPC of ~$2–3** while learning |

## 2. Keywords (paste into the ad group)

Use these match types exactly — the brackets and quotes matter:

```
[built with ai canada]
[ai consulting canada]
"ai consultant canada"
"ai consulting regina"
"ai consulting saskatchewan"
"custom ai apps canada"
"ai automation for business canada"
"ai agency canada"
```

**Negative keywords** (add these so you don't pay for irrelevant clicks):

```
jobs
course
free course
tutorial
salary
what is ai
```

## 3. Responsive Search Ad copy

Add these as separate **Headlines** (Google mixes them; you can add up to 15):

- Built with AI — Canadian AI Studio
- AI Consulting Across Canada
- Custom AI Apps & Automation
- Practical AI for Your Business
- Free AI Opportunity Audit
- Agentic Workflows, Built for You
- From AI Idea to Working Tool
- Book a Free AI Audit Today

**Descriptions** (add up to 4):

- Canadian AI studio building custom apps, agentic workflows, and automations around how your business actually works.
- Not hype — practical AI adoption. Book a free AI Opportunity Audit and get a clear next-step plan.
- Custom apps, AI agents, and business automation for Canadian companies. Remote-friendly, coast to coast.
- Turn AI potential into real workflows. Free audit, no obligation, response within 24 hours.

**Final URL:** `https://builtwithai.ca/ai-consulting-canada.html`
**Display path:** `builtwithai.ca/Canada` (fields: `Canada` / `AI-Audit`)

## 4. After launch

- Add **sitelink extensions** pointing to `#services`, `#process`, `#faq`, and the case study page.
- Add a **call/lead form extension** if you want phone or direct form leads.
- Check the **Search Terms report** after a few days and add any junk terms as negatives.
- A `[built with ai canada]` exact-match keyword in a small market like this is usually **cheap** — often well under $2/click.

---

**Bottom line:** Ads = visible on Google today (paid). SEO = visible on Google in
1–3 weeks (free, compounding). Run both: the ad buys you time while the organic
rankings catch up.
